FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the European Accessibility Act (EAA)?

It's EU legislation requiring many digital services — including online stores serving EU customers — to meet accessibility standards. AllyFix helps you align with the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines it's based on.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. AllyFix installs from the Shopify App Store in one click and runs inside your Shopify admin. Fixes are applied for you.

Will it change how my store looks?

Fixes are designed to preserve your design. For example, contrast corrections find the closest compliant color, and image descriptions are added invisibly for screen readers.

How does the AI write image descriptions?

Vision AI actually looks at each product image and describes what's in it — so the alt text is accurate and useful, not a generic label.

How am I billed?

Through Shopify's secure billing, right on your Shopify invoice. Start with a 7-day free trial and cancel anytime.
